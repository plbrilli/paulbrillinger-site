Founder & CEO

OuiSi Inc., Brooklyn

Disconnection, from each other and our environment, is the defining problem of our time, and OuiSi approaches it creatively: a physical-meets-digital platform that lives in homes, classrooms and memory-care centers. It continues today, restructured and leaner by design.

Built to $15M of revenue from nothing, and into a quarter-million homes and classrooms.
