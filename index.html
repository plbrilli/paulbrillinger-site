Since 2015

Founder & CEO

OuiSi Inc., Brooklyn

Disconnection, from each other and our environment, is the defining problem of our time, and OuiSi approaches this problem creatively.

OuiSi is a physical-meets-digital product platform that fosters creativity, curiosity and connection. Built through partnerships with the Getty, Boston MFA, the Smithsonian, and Paramount, OuiSi lives in homes, classrooms, and memory care centers across the country.

The company continues today, restructured and leaner by design. While it's no longer a full-time job, I'm using free time to expand OuiSi's digital footprint at home, in the classroom and in therapeutic settings.
