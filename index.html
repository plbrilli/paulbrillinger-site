Brooklyn & the Hudson Valley · Strategy · New ventures · Product

For many founders, building a company can be surprisingly or subconsciously personal. What they are building often fills in for an unmet need, or scratches an existential itch they can't quite name. Between that and being too close to the company's biggest problems, they need help finding the right angle to overcome obstacles.

That's been my work twice over. Global macro investing at Wellington and Point72, then OuiSi, a company I built from a thesis about human connection.

How I work.

Both halves are connection, pointed in two directions. One at the people, to find the why. One at the ideas, to find the how.

i.

I work with people to understand how their needs and ways of working have led to current challenges. Usually that means reconnecting a team with why they started. A venture runs on more than a plan. It runs on what drives the team.

ii.

I use my analytical and creative background to help find a new angle on a seemingly exhausted effort. I've come up with ideas, made products, run a P&L, and negotiated partnerships.

Teams rarely lack effort, but they may be forcing things from the wrong perspective.

What you're left with.

A deeper self-awareness as it relates to the company.
A new understanding of, and approach to, a problem.
A revitalized sense of creativity and purpose.

Where I'm most useful.

A company with traction that's lost the thread.

You've proven something works. I help discover how it should evolve, often by getting back to the "why" behind it.

A product that needs inventing, not just specifying.

Someone has to help make the thing, not write a brief and hand it off.

A founder carrying too much alone.

Sometimes what helps most is a clear, like-minded head across the table with no stake in being right.

Where that comes from.

The thread through all of it is connection. Making connections, finding connections, noticing connections.

In markets that meant creative manipulation of data that no one thought to put side by side. At OuiSi it meant building something that fosters connectedness: with the world around us, with each other, with our inner selves.

Now, it's addressing problems that require a fresh angle, human understanding, curiosity and flexibility. Between ideas that don't obviously belong together, between people, between digital and physical.

Deep inside a problem? I'd like to hear about it.
